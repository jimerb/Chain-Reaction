    THE OPERATOR’S HANDBOOK

    Run the reactor.

    Build a matching chain with five dice. Every extra roll could raise your score—or end the turn with nothing. You choose when to bank.

    Your first turn

    1. 01
      Roll five dice.

      Choose any group of at least two matching dice. Its value is the number on the dice multiplied by how many you hold.

    2. 02
      Choose your pairing.

      You decide which group to keep when more than one appears. The game never picks for you.

    3. 03
      Bank or roll again.

      Bank the chain’s points and end your turn, or roll the remaining dice to try to improve it.

    OPENING SPLIT
    22266

    Choose three 2s for 6 points or two 6s for 12 points. The group you leave behind can still matter on a later roll.

    After another roll

    Extend

    New dice matching your held chain join it. You can bank the larger chain or roll the remaining dice.

    Switch

    A new pair of a different number can become your chain. You give up the old unbanked points, and those old dice return to the rolling pool. Then bank or roll again.

    Choose

    If the roll both matches your chain and makes a different pair, the game shows both options. You decide which strand to pursue.

    Miss

    If there is no chain match and no new pair, an available rescue enhancement may help. Otherwise, a meltdown scores 0 for this turn. Earlier banked points stay safe.

    Example: Hold two 2s, then roll 3, 3, 3. Switching makes a chain of three 3s worth 9. The old 2s return, so you may bank 9 or roll those two dice. Their old faces count again only after they are rerolled. A missed chain cannot simply be kept or banked after the roll.

    Reactor controls

    Each enhancement can be used once per player per game, and only one can be used on a given roll. A lit LED means unused; it may still be temporarily unavailable. Hover over or focus its Engage button for the exact rule and reason.

    E

    Enrichment

    Change one eligible die from the latest roll to your chain’s number. Before choosing an opening chain, change it to another number already showing to form or improve a pair. Held dice and just-released dice are not eligible.

    Ⅱ

    Control Rod

    After a roll misses your held chain, bank the points it had before that roll and end your turn. It works even if a different pair appeared; it cannot rescue an opening leak.

    F

    Fusion

    Bank two different matching strands and end your turn: a held chain of at least two plus a newly rolled pair or larger group of another number. Fresh matches to the held chain count too; unrelated single dice do not.

    Fusion example: Hold 5, 5 and roll 6, 6, 2. Bank 10 + 12 = 22; the 2 scores nothing. Rolling 6, 2, 3 instead gives no second pair, so Fusion is unavailable. On an opening split, choose one pair first, then you may fuse the other.

    Enrichment and Fusion open a chooser. Cancel or Escape closes it without spending the enhancement.

    How a game ends

    Radiation leak

    Five different dice on the opening roll cost up to 10 banked points, with a floor of zero. Enrichment can make a pair before the penalty.

    Critical Mass

    Five matching dice automatically score their face value × 5 and end the turn. A pair, triple, or four of a kind does not.

    Final round

    When someone reaches the target, finish the round so everyone gets equal turns. Highest total wins. Tied leaders alone play extra rounds.

    Read the odds

    The displayed survival chance counts either a chain match or a replacement pair. It excludes enhancements, and switching might lower your points.
